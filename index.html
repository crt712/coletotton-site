Cole Totton

Founder, Pristine Clean Club & Enablr — Charlotte, NC

Cole Totton is an entrepreneur and company builder based in Charlotte, North Carolina. After a career with Fortune 50 financial institutions, he left corporate finance to build and operate businesses of his own. His primary focus is Pristine Clean Club LLC, a consumer goods company. Through Pristine Clean Club, he is working to innovate in fabric care, an industry that has largely emphasized convenience for decades. He also develops ventures spanning technology and business services.
